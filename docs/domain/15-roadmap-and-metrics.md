## Part 15 — Domain Roadmap and Measurement

### 15.1 Four phases

**Phase 1 — Validate supply and demand.** Run the Part 14 MVP in a local area with a manageable supplier cohort. Measure repeated truthful publication, accepted commitments, actual pickups, time to first reservation, resolved failure reasons, and operating effort. Expand only when pickups occur reliably and outstanding consumer returns are controlled. Publication volume alone is not success.

**Phase 2 — Increase recurrence.** Introduce confirmed recurrence, scheduled windows, interests/follows, selective alerts, and progressive pricing. Compare repeated supplier participation and actual pickup gains against alert fatigue, phantom stock, and no-show changes. Require today's actual quantity confirmation before any recurring offer exists.

**Phase 3 — Improve monetization.** Introduce verified high-value Pro benefits and transparent fees only after repeat usage. Observe supplier retention, fulfilled-value economics, small-offer viability, refund funding, and whether monetization harms free reuse. Upgrade/downgrade rules protect existing commitments.

**Phase 4 — Build a local reuse network.** Add wanted-surplus demand, multiple local points, authorized organizations, and validated material reuse relationships. Measure matching-to-pickup success, broader useful categories, participation, and substantiated reuse. Do not expand into ordinary secondhand retail merely to increase listing counts.

### 15.2 Metric definitions

Every result includes observation interval, local time zone, category/area where applicable, sample size, eligible population, and evidence basis. Separate offer cohorts by first publication time from transaction cohorts by final pickup or outcome time. Never mix an unfinished cohort into a resolved-outcome rate without labeling it.

- **Published offers:** distinct offers first Published during the interval. Scheduled but unopened offers are counted separately; resumes do not count twice.
- **Fulfilled offers:** published offers with at least one final handover. Report Fully Fulfilled when all admitted non-withdrawn units were handed over, and Partially Fulfilled otherwise. Explicit supplier withdrawals remain visible so withdrawals cannot inflate a success claim.
- **Offer pickup conversion:** published offers with a handover divided by published offers in a booking-closed cohort, with open pickup outcomes separately pending.
- **Reservation pickup conversion:** final-handover reservations divided by accepted reservations in a resolved cohort; also show free, online, local-payment, and unpaid-commitment outcomes separately.
- **Discovery-to-reservation conversion:** identified offer-detail viewing sessions with at least one accepted commitment divided by eligible offer-detail viewing sessions in the same window. Report only when those observations exist and the counting unit is declared.
- **Time to first accepted reservation:** first accepted commitment time minus first publication time. Report distributions and the share with no commitment; do not pretend an unreserved offer took zero time.
- **Quantity reused:** actual handed-over sellable units, with item-equivalent counts only when contents are known. Never sum heterogeneous units into a fabricated common item measure.
- **Gross paid value:** substantiated received payments, with local acknowledgments separately labeled; duplicates/excess and returns excluded from fulfilled-value reporting.
- **Fulfilled paid value:** accepted value of units actually handed over, corrected for approved post-pickup restitution; free goods contribute zero monetary value.
- **Supplier revenue recovered:** fulfilled paid value minus refunds/restitution, supplier-borne provider costs, and platform fees. Report gross fulfilled value alongside net recovery so costs are transparent.
- **Consumer money saved:** credible ordinary price for the actual handed-over contents minus accepted goods price, with later restitution separately identified. If no credible ordinary-price basis exists, report savings Unknown, not zero or a fabricated discount.
- **Offer expiry share:** offers whose booking ended by Expired divided by booking-closed published offers in the cohort. Also report zero-pickup expiry and expired-unit share; booking expiry can coexist with successful later pickups.
- **Free publication share:** offers initially published at zero divided by published offers; report later free-stage offers separately.
- **Free fulfilled-unit share:** free handed-over units divided by all handed-over units within a comparable category/unit basis.
- **Consumer no-show rate:** resolved No-Show outcomes divided by completed pickups plus resolved consumer No-Shows. Pending attribution and supplier failures are excluded and disclosed.
- **Supplier failure rate:** attributable failed/cancelled reservations divided by completed handovers plus attributable supplier failures, with partial shortfall quantity additionally reported.
- **Quantity pickup rate:** handed-over quantity divided by confirmed allocated quantity in the resolved cohort. Pending temporary commitments are separate; releases and partial cancellations are shown.
- **Supplier cancellation count/rate:** supplier-attributed cancellations with a defined resolved-confirmed-reservation denominator; exclude consumer cancellations and neutral force majeure.
- **Consumer cancellation count:** cancellations split by before-payment, after-payment, free/local mode, and timely/late; no invented fault rate for unresolved cases.
- **Consumer completed pickups:** distinct final handovers, including partial final outcomes once.
- **Consumer free pickups:** distinct final handovers under accepted Free mode, including genuinely free donated units; a later refund of a paid purchase is not a free reservation.
- **Return performance:** approved outstanding amount, confirmed returned amount, time to initiation/completion, blocked cases, and named responsible funder.
- **Supplier repeat participation:** suppliers with truthful publication in successive declared intervals, paired with actual pickups and failure rates.

### 15.3 Impact: fact, declaration, and estimate

Use three explicit evidence labels:

1. **Measured/verified outcome:** actual handed-over units; measured weight when a documented measurement exists; substantiated online receipt/return; jointly acknowledged handover.
2. **Declared information:** supplier's product weight, ordinary price, intended disposal, age/condition declaration, or locally acknowledged payment. State who declared it and its basis.
3. **Estimate:** calculated weight, volume, savings, or avoided-disposal claim using disclosed assumptions and confidence limits. State method and uncertainty; never present it as exact measured impact.

Report reused units and measured/declared/estimated weight separately. A bag of unknown contents counts as one collected bag, not an invented number of food items. Mixed material weights may be summed only in a common physical unit and with the evidence classes visible; estimates do not become measured totals through aggregation.

Avoided disposal is an impact inference: actual pickup plus a credible declared disposal intention supports **estimated disposal avoided**, not proof of what would have happened otherwise. Supplier recovery is a financial outcome, not a weight measure. Savings requires a credible ordinary-price comparison for actual received contents; a claimed “surprise value” needs a defensible basis. Free goods may show supported estimated savings, but not unsupported market values.

Do not derive carbon, water, or environmental-equivalence claims without a separately substantiated method. A cancellation, refunded non-pickup, unconfirmed reservation, or fabricated handover contributes no reuse impact. Corrections adjust aggregates and retain their cause.
