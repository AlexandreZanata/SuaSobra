## Part 16 — Edge Cases and Expected Behavior

These 60 scenarios are business acceptance cases. Each specifies the expected commercial result without prescribing an implementation.

### Quantity, eligibility, and publication

- **EDGE-001 — Two consumers request the last unit simultaneously.** Exactly one accepted commitment receives it. The other request fails without payment entitlement, allocated stock, or a no-show record. Priority is acceptance, not first page view. See INV-004–006.
- **EDGE-002 — A consumer requests four units when only three are available.** Reject the requested quantity and explain the current maximum. Do not silently substitute three; a fresh acceptance is needed.
- **EDGE-003 — A supplier attempts to reduce five admitted units to one while two are committed.** Reject any change that erases committed stock. Withdraw only current available units; if two units do not physically exist, declare explicit shortage and cancel/refund the affected commitments.
- **EDGE-004 — A cancelled reservation releases the same unit twice.** The first valid cancellation disposes of the allocation. Repeating the action has no further quantity, refund, allowance, or reputation effect.
- **EDGE-005 — A supplier adds genuine surplus after the offer sells out.** Increase admitted and available quantity together if booking is still open and the sellable unit is identical. Published/Sold Out becomes an appropriate available label; previous commitments remain fixed.
- **EDGE-006 — A supplier publishes ordinary new retail stock with a large discount.** Require a genuine surplus circumstance. Discount alone is insufficient; refuse publication or remove an ineligible offer, handling live commitments explicitly if any exist.
- **EDGE-007 — A used business pallet is surplus, but its previous chemical exposure is unknown.** Do not market it as safe for food or sensitive use. If relevant contamination risk cannot be excluded under the category policy, refuse publication; truthful surplus status does not resolve safety.
- **EDGE-008 — A supplier keeps republishing the same missing item.** New publication cannot erase prior failure evidence or restore old commitments. Investigate actual availability, stop phantom publication, remedy consumers, and apply fraud/abuse sanctions when substantiated.
- **EDGE-009 — An approved supplier loses publication eligibility just before a scheduled opening.** Do not open booking. Pause for review or cancel with an explicit reason; no phantom public stock, and no automatic allowance restoration unless a verified platform error justifies a credit.
- **EDGE-010 — A mixed construction lot is advertised as 13 tiles, 17 blocks, and a remaining bag of mortar.** It is one sellable unit with disclosed condition and contents. One tile cannot be reserved separately; a material shortage makes the promised unit unavailable rather than automatically divisible.

### Price, timing, and payment

- **EDGE-011 — Price drops while the consumer is viewing the offer.** A new acceptance uses the effective current price. If displayed and current terms differ, obtain acceptance of the current terms; no stock or price lock exists from viewing.
- **EDGE-012 — Price drops during payment for an already accepted commitment.** Preserve its accepted amount and original deadline. The consumer may cancel and try a new reservation, but receives no automatic price adjustment or preferential claim.
- **EDGE-013 — The supplier raises price after a reservation is confirmed.** Only future uncommitted units can use the higher price under the alteration policy. The confirmed reservation keeps its original price, quantity, and pickup terms.
- **EDGE-014 — A progressive stage turns the offer free while some reservations are paid.** New commitments use Free mode and its caps/deadline. Existing paid or payment-pending commitments retain their original mode and amount; no automatic refund solely for the new stage.
- **EDGE-015 — A progressive schedule would increase from BRL 5.00 to BRL 8.00.** Reject the schedule as non-progressive. A supplier can cancel the schedule and make a separately disclosed prospective manual price change, without altering prior commitments.
- **EDGE-016 — A payment is confirmed after its reservation expires.** Do not revive the entitlement even if stock appears available or the payer initiated payment earlier. Record the late receipt and full refund obligation; a new claim needs new current-term acceptance.
- **EDGE-017 — Payment is recognized exactly at its deadline.** The end boundary is exclusive, so the commitment has ended. Treat received funds as late money and return them; no backdated priority.
- **EDGE-018 — The offer booking interval closes while online payment is pending.** End the pending commitment at that boundary, move its quantity to expired/withdrawn as appropriate, and return any later receipt. Confirmed reservations with later accepted pickup deadlines remain valid.
- **EDGE-019 — A payment attempt fails with time still remaining.** Permit a new attempt only after the failed attempt is definitively ended, under the same obligation and original deadline. No extra hold duration or quantity is created.
- **EDGE-020 — The consumer pays twice for the same obligation.** Confirm one timely matching settlement, reserve/fulfill once, and return duplicate money. Distinguish two actual receipts from repeated recognition of one receipt.
- **EDGE-021 — The consumer pays less than the accepted total.** Partial settlement is unsupported in the MVP. End the unpaid obligation/commitment and return the received amount; do not grant partial stock ownership or permit an informal top-up.
- **EDGE-022 — The consumer pays more than the accepted total.** A substantiated timely receipt may satisfy only the original total. Refund excess and preserve original quantity; overpayment never buys extra units automatically.
- **EDGE-023 — A payment screenshot appears successful but Asaas receipt is unconfirmed.** Do not confirm pickup entitlement. Use the screenshot for investigation and recognize only substantiated financial receipt; deadline and late-refund rules still apply.
- **EDGE-024 — Asaas or supplier financial standing is restricted before a new online publication.** Do not offer online mode. An eligible supplier may create a separate future free/local-payment publication; existing online payments and promised returns remain assigned for resolution.
- **EDGE-025 — The supplier demands cash for a still-pending online reservation.** Cash cannot satisfy that online obligation. Refuse the informal mode switch, investigate diversion, and explain the existing financial position before any new commercial agreement.
- **EDGE-026 — A refund is approved but available financial funds are insufficient.** Leave it Approved or Failed/In Progress with outstanding reimbursement amount and responsible funder. Arrange funding and escalate; do not show Refunded or deduct the shortage from the consumer's entitlement.
- **EDGE-027 — A financial dispute and a refund concern the same money.** Reconcile returned, disputed, and still-outstanding amounts. Prevent double recovery while preserving the consumer's entitled amount and the recorded physical pickup.
- **EDGE-028 — A payment confirmation is recognized after supplier cancellation.** The entitlement has ended. Record and return the money; supplier cancellation cannot become a live pickup again because payment arrived.
- **EDGE-029 — Only one minute remains before online booking closes.** Reject a new online commitment because it cannot meet the two-minute minimum. An already accepted live commitment uses its own original deadline; do not invent extra time.
- **EDGE-030 — An online obligation receives money in a different currency.** Do not satisfy a BRL obligation with an unexplained currency conversion. Open a financial reconciliation case and return substantiated funds through a valid remedy; no pickup right arises.

### Pickup, absence, and safety

- **EDGE-031 — The consumer arrives and the product has already been given to someone else.** Record supplier failure, cancel unfulfilled entitlement, refund paid uncollected quantity, and investigate the quantity inconsistency. The punctual consumer receives no no-show penalty.
- **EDGE-032 — The supplier cancels a paid reservation shortly before collection.** Cancel explicitly and communicate immediately; refund its full uncollected amount. Attribute supplier failure unless a substantiated neutral cause applies; do not require the consumer to negotiate privately.
- **EDGE-033 — The establishment closes before pickup.** Stop new commitments at that point and cancel or resolve failed live pickups. Return uncollected payments and assess supplier/neutral responsibility; do not penalize consumer absence at a closed site.
- **EDGE-034 — A consumer arrives during the ten-minute grace period.** Allow pickup only before the accepted effective deadline, with supplier service still available and no hard safety limit exceeded. An available grace period is part of the accepted terms, not a guarantee of unsafe extension.
- **EDGE-035 — A consumer arrives after the effective pickup deadline.** The old entitlement has ended. Do not revive it automatically; a fresh eligible offer/reservation is necessary if goods remain safely available, and already released units cannot be reclaimed.
- **EDGE-036 — A consumer arrives before pickup opens.** Do not hand over under the baseline. Explain the accepted opening time; early arrival does not consume stock or create a no-show if later timely collection occurs.
- **EDGE-037 — A nominated third party collects the reservation.** Verify nomination and the unused confirmation. Complete one handover for the owner; the collector gains no unrelated cancellation or financial authority.
- **EDGE-038 — Two collectors present the same valid reference.** One final handover is possible. Once completed, the second attempt is refused regardless of whether both were previously nominated or know the same reference.
- **EDGE-039 — Consumer reserved three identical baskets, but only two are available.** If the consumer agrees, perform one final handover of two units, cancel the remaining unit, and refund its original value. Record supplier shortfall. The third unit cannot be collected later under the same reservation.
- **EDGE-040 — A surprise bag contains fewer goods than its minimum promise.** Consumer may reject the unit and receive a full return of any paid amount, or submit a substantiated complaint if already collected. The supplier cannot redefine the promised minimum after acceptance.
- **EDGE-041 — A surprise bag has unknown allergens incompatible with a declared safety promise.** Do not conceal the uncertainty or force acceptance. Stop the affected fulfillment and refund rejected paid quantity; investigate the unsupported safety statement.
- **EDGE-042 — Food becomes unsafe after payment but before pickup.** Withdraw it and cancel affected entitlements; refund uncollected payments in full. Payment and consumer consent cannot make unsafe food collectible.
- **EDGE-043 — A free offer is food whose hard safety limit ends before normal grace.** The hard limit determines the latest handover. No price, goodwill extension, or free status allows collection beyond it; end the entitlement and attribute fairly.
- **EDGE-044 — A consumer misses a free reservation.** End entitlement, release only eligible stock, and resolve responsibility. Record no-show only after confirmed supplier readiness and the challenge opportunity; apply the same proportional progression without inventing a fee.
- **EDGE-045 — A reservation expires while awaiting payment.** Treat it as unpaid commitment expiry, not missed attendance. Release/dispose once and return any exceptional later money; no no-show sanction.
- **EDGE-046 — The consumer claims timely attendance, while the supplier claims absence.** End entitlement at the deadline, protect applicable refund entitlement, and retain Outcome Under Review. Resolve evidence before any no-show/supplier-failure consequence; unresolved responsibility is not an automatic consumer fault.
- **EDGE-047 — A supplier verifies the reference, but the consumer refuses materially different goods.** Verification alone is not pickup. Record failed fulfillment, preserve zero handed-over quantity, and refund paid rejected quantity when the mismatch is substantiated.
- **EDGE-048 — An incorrect pickup record is discovered after the unit was reallocated or a refund completed.** Do not automatically rewrite stock or create a second handover. Dual-authorized operations reconciles the original evidence, quantity, and money through an explicit correction case.
- **EDGE-049 — Force majeure prevents both parties from meeting.** End obligations safely, return uncollected paid value, and record Neutral attribution where established. Do not fabricate either consumer no-show or supplier negligence.
- **EDGE-050 — The supplier changes pickup address after commitments exist.** The original point remains protected. If fulfillment there is impossible, cancel/refund rather than imposing a move; any new point requires a fresh offer/acceptance.

### Moderation, fairness, growth, and reporting

- **EDGE-051 — A moderator removes an offer with free, unpaid, and paid commitments.** Stop new booking, cancel affected live entitlements, withdraw affected quantity, end unpaid obligations, and create returns for received uncollected payments. Retain completed pickup history and assess post-pickup remedies separately.
- **EDGE-052 — A new consumer with no reputation requests free surplus.** Permit access subject to ordinary account, quantity, timing, and simultaneous limits. No prior rating is required; display New instead of a misleading zero score.
- **EDGE-053 — Two household members share an address and both reserve.** Shared residence alone is not multiple-account abuse. Respect independent eligible accounts unless corroborated control/evasion evidence supports a reasoned restriction.
- **EDGE-054 — A consumer has three live commitments and requests another.** Reject until one ends; pending online commitments count. A completed pickup frees the simultaneous slot but continues to count toward that offer's cumulative quantity cap.
- **EDGE-055 — A supplier tries to review a cancelled transaction or creates a self-purchase.** Deny review eligibility and self-reservation. Incident reporting remains available for genuine cancelled transactions; self-dealing attempts are assessed for abuse.
- **EDGE-056 — A user reports an offer solely to retaliate against a negative review.** Investigate the report's substance without assuming it true or false from timing alone. Substantiated retaliatory fabrication receives its own sanction; legitimate original review rights stay protected.
- **EDGE-057 — Supplier downgrades while live reservations and price schedules exist.** Preserve accepted entitlements and finish already approved schedules. Apply lower future publication benefits at period end; pause future recurring creation if its entitlement is lost.
- **EDGE-058 — A bakery's recurrence template is active, but today's quantity is not confirmed.** Publish nothing. Do not assume yesterday's leftovers, reuse yesterday's safety limits, or consume new allowance for a nonexistent confirmed occurrence.
- **EDGE-059 — A wanted-surplus request matches an offer that sells out first.** Matching creates no right to the stock. Keep the demand active until fulfilled/cancelled/expired, and suggest other eligible offers without inventing priority.
- **EDGE-060 — A supplier claims that one unknown-content bag saved 5 kg and BRL 100.00.** Count one verified collected bag. Label substantiated declared measurements/price basis appropriately; unsupported weight and savings remain Unknown and do not enter measured impact totals.
